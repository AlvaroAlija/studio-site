Every paid render in the store includes the scene file. You can relight it, re-frame it, strip the geometry out and use it in your own work, commercial included.

What you cannot do is resell the pack itself. That is the whole licence, and it fits in two sentences on purpose.

```text
Included:  render.png  ·  scene.blend  ·  textures/  ·  LICENCE.txt
```
